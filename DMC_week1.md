## Homework

  - Read: **Preface for DMC Course** as [web page](DMC_preface.html) or as  [PDF](DMC_preface.pdf).
       - The preface ends with a section "An Arc of Stories and Lenses", which has a subsection for each of the four tales we will study.
	   -  Part of the homework for each week will be to read my notes on the story for the week.  These notes will always start with the
	       summary given in the preface.
  - Read: [Notes on *Notes from the Underground*](DMC_notes_on_notes.html) 
      - As noted above, the first section of these notes also appear in the preface.
      - If you are a person who likes to experience a story before external influences try to shape your thinking on it, you may wish to
	     read these notes after watching the one man show.
  - Watch ["Notes from the Underground", one man show starring Larry Cedar, free on youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=asp5tqDql0g)  
     ![ ](images/notes_from_underground_cedar.jpg){width=50%}  <!-- image from https://img.youtube.com/vi/asp5tqDql0g/hqdefault.jpg -->
