## [optional, use only if they help] Questions to Guide Reflection


### Questions about the story

- Why is the story called "The Perfect Match"?
- How hard was it, in December 2012, to foresee a world so relavant to our lives now?
- If Tilly only ever gave Sai what he wanted, how would he tell the difference between a need that was his and one that was given to him?
- How did the system plan ahead for dealing with people who try to defy its form of mind control?

### Questions relating to week 3 class discussion

- How does reading a story compare to watching a film?
- How useful is polemical fiction, which focuses on ideas and often leads to flat caricatures? 

### Question  from the Preface Section on "The Perfect Match":

- Can you relate to how the protagonist is controlled through false needs?
- Can you relate to how our independence is limited because corporations are always observing us and trying to influence us?
- How important is privacy to the main character, and to you?
