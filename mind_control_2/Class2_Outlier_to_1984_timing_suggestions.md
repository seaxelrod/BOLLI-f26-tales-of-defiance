title: "Class2 Outlier to 1984 timing suggestions"
---


Here's a suggested plan. It puts about 35 minutes on the questions, with the setup slides moving quickly.

We still keep in mind Dwight Eisenhower's quote: "Plans are useless, but planning is essential."
Applied to the timeline, it meant that, of course, we will adapt on the fly, rather than follow the plan.

I encourage you to defy my mathematican's efficiency  (see D-503 in "We") and emphaisze thinking, rather than passively receiving  thoughts.

- **0:00–0:04 · Opening (slides 1–3: Title, Road Map, Table of Context), 4 min**
  - Use the timeline on the title slide to state tonight's arc in one sentence.
  - Go over the table quickly and don't stop on each cell. It's there for reference.
- **0:04–0:07 · *Notes from Underground*: the outlier ignored (slide 4), 3 min**
  - A quick recap of last week.
- **0:07–0:12 · Stepping stones: *We* and *Brave New World* (slide 5), 5 min**
  - Most people haven't read them, so present this slide rather than discussing it.
- **0:12–0:18 · Disciplinary Surveillance (slide 6), 6 min**
  - Includes about 2 minutes for one clip. The telescreen reveal is the strongest.
- **0:18–0:23 · Narrative Analysis (slide 7), 5 min**
  - Includes about 1–2 minutes for the diary clip.
- **0:23–0:26 · Bruner's terms (slide 8), 3 min**
  - Cut this first if you're running late, since the questions raise the same ideas.
- **0:26–0:32 · Fromm's Afterword (slide 9), 6 min**
  - Includes about 2 minutes for one clip, the boot line or Room 101 but not both.
- **0:32–0:34 · Control dressed as care (slide 10), 2 min**
  - A short bridge into the questions.
- **0:34–1:09 · Questions for the room (slide 11), 35 min**
  - Plan on going deep into three or four questions, not all six, at about 8–10 minutes each.
  - Good openers are #1 (lonelier) and #4 (are you an outlier?). They are personal and easy to answer.
  - Keep #5 (Fromm) and #6 (history and truth) for the second half. Those are the ones most likely to turn to current events.
- **1:09–1:19 · Orwell in the news (slide 12), 10 min**
  - Pick two or three of the extra-credit finds and ask which lens each one uses.
  - If #6 already drifted into current events, merge it with this slide.
- **1:19–1:22 · What comes next: *The Matrix* (slide 13), 3 min**
- **1:22–1:30 · Buffer, 8 min**
  - For overruns, a second clip, or a question that takes off.

**Clips:** budget about 6–8 minutes of film in total. The Scene Index (slide 14) is there if a discussion calls for another scene.

**If you're running long:** cut Bruner (slide 8) and Control as Care (slide 10) before you cut question time.
