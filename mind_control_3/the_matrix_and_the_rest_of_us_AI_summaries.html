No One Is Coming The Matrix and the Rest of Us

  • Fear or desire? 1984 controls people through fear; The Matrix controls them through comfort. Most of us live with a mix of both.
  • Flawed people, not heroes: the Underground Man and Winston are compromised and they fail. We learn from them by recognizing ourselves in them.
  • Neo is different: he is the chosen One, with echoes of Jesus and the Buddha, plus bullet time. Ours is the only tale of defiance in the course that needs a savior.
  • Room for the rest of us: for most of the film Neo doesn’t believe he’s the One. He starts out uncertain and unchosen, like the rest of us.
  • Cypher, the Winston of The Matrix: tired, missing the taste of steak, he makes a deal to go back to sleep. He may be the character we understand best.
  • No red pill for us: our choices are smaller. If resistance lives in Deleuze’s “circuit breakers,” the work of staying human falls to each of us, a little at a time.
