Overview & Themes

Breakdown of name:

  • “Tales of”
  • “Defiance to”
  • “Mind Control”
  • “in the Modern Age”

Perspective:

  • Each week we will see a different kind of societally imposed mind control.
  • We will discuss how each story presents a specific realization of a general pattern by which this type of mind control works.
  • This will help clarify how this type of control is still relevant today.
  • Class will discuss how the stories move them and reflect on how participants understand, work within, or rebel against this type of control.

We will see how the stories, taken together form an arc of increasingly sophisticated dytopias. The old dystopias are still present.

  • People are still rebellig against The Enlightenment, as the Undeground Man did in Notes from the Undeground.
    • And there are still people …
  • People are still rebelling against The Ministry of Truth, as Winston Smith did in 1984.
  • And there are still “villains” trying to own and manipuate Truth as big brother did.
  • People are still rebelling against a vritual fake reality, as Neo did in The Matrix.
    • And there are still people trying to force us into their created reality, as the Architects did.
  • People are still rebelling against companies controlling us by telling us what we want, as Sai did in The Perefect Match.
    • And there are still companies that control us, like Centillion.

Increasingly sophisticated, multi-layered arc of stories and lenses.
