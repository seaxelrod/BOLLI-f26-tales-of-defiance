Questions to Guide Reflection

These are optional. Use them only as they help you.

Questions about the story

  • Why is the story called “The Perfect Match”?
  • If Tilly only ever gave Sai what he wanted, how would he tell the difference between a need that was his and one that was given to him?
  • How did the system plan ahead for dealing with people who try to defy its form of mind control?
  • How hard was it, in December 2012, to foresee a world so relavant to our lives now?

Questions relating to week 3 class discussion

  • How does reading a story compare to watching a film?
  • How useful is polemical fiction, which focuses on ideas and often leads to flat caricatures?

Question from the Preface Section on “The Perfect Match”:

  • Can you relate to how the protagonist is controlled through false needs?
  • Can you relate to how our independence is limited because corporations are always observing us and trying to influence us?
  • How important is privacy to the main character, and to you?

Questions from week three presentation

  • How did you absorb “The Perfect Match”?
  • Are we being controlled by a false sense of what we need?
  • What does defiance look like when control no longer feels like control?
  • What keeps you human?
  • How do you resist systems built to monitor, predict, and please you?
  • How would you add the story to the context table in the presentation?
    • Or you could add it as a fourth point to the one-sentence summaries in “Telly Helps Prepare …”
      1. Dostoyevsky’s Underground Man being ignored as he is spiteful against 2+2=4 rational egoism
      2. Orwell’s Winston Smith’s being crushed as he merely tried to remember or even contemplate a world without Big Brother’s disciplinary surveillance and Orwell’s Newspeak narrative controls
      3. the Wachowski’s Neo who the AI agents try to erase before he can pull humanity out of a prison in a modern Plato’s cave - a society for control manufactured by AI

Question in “Tilly Helps Prepare a Defiance to Mind Control Class on the”The Perfect Match”

  • How to handle all this stuff in a human and humane way?
  • Would we be losing something essential about our humanity if we take the easy, comfortable route?
