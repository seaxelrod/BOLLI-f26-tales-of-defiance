**Method:** The inflation adjustment is CPI-U October 2026 ÷ CPI-U December 2012. Each 2012 market cap is the historical year-end figure multiplied by 1.470; results are rounded to the nearest $0.1 billion. The 2026 values and ranks are a November 21 market snapshot, so they can change with share prices. 

The 2012 ranks for Apple, China Mobile, Samsung, and Microsoft are their recorded positions in a year-end ranking. The other 2012 rank bands are rough estimates, not verified exact ranks; historical rankings can differ with their treatment of share classes and free float. SpaceX was private in 2012, so it had no public market-cap rank then.
