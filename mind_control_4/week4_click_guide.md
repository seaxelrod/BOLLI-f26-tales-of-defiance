Each quote link on the slides opens the highlighted story with that passage boxed in purple. Other purple passages within about a screen of it come along for free. This guide groups the links by those on-screen clusters and suggests, frame by frame, whether a click is worth the time.

Frame numbers are the lower-right numbers in Presentation Draft 8.

# The four clusters worth a click

These are the scenes where one click shows several purple passages at once. If time allows only a few clicks, these are the ones.

1. **The date scene.** Bored, the dessert suggestion, *"I'm being treated like a child,"* *"Gaps in sharing…"* One click shows Sai's whole turn from contentment to switching Tilly off. Reachable from four frames (4, 8, 9, 11), so show it once and refer back to it afterward.
2. **Jenny's apartment debate.** *"You mean some advertiser paid Centillion to pitch it at you,"* Sai's *"the perfect product finds the perfect consumer,"* and the Wi-Fi documents. Both lenses argue it out on one screen. From frame 9.
3. **The capture.** "We Are the Champions," *"Tilly, call the police!" "I'm afraid I can't do that, Sai,"* and white tea with the founder. Control dressed as care, served on a tray. From frame 11.
4. **Rinn's offer.** Cyborgs, machines thinking for us, the job offer, "the perfect match," "any replacement is likely worse," and "adapt," all within a screen or two. From frame 12; one click, then scroll.

Two smaller pairs are also worth a click when the discussion is there: **the ending** ("My Way" and the blinking red light, frame 13) and **the betrayal** (*"just like they accuse us of doing,"* *"I was surprised by you,"* *"We unveil only the darkness…"*, frame 4).

# Frame by frame

## Frame 2: Listen (Vivaldi)

- The box plays the music; no story click needed.
- **Suggestion:** play a few seconds while people settle, or skip.

## Frame 3: Four Wake-Up Songs

- Each song links to its morning in the story.
- "Il Sospetto" stands alone. "So What" opens the morning Tilly comes back (the chill, the twelve-hour gap). "We Are the Champions" opens the capture scene. "My Way" opens the ending.
- **Suggestion:** read the four descriptions; don't click. The frame's job is to bring the plot back quickly. If the class wants a scene, the Champions click doubles as the capture cluster.

## Frame 4: Emotional Arc of Sai

- **Bored** or **Irritated** opens the date scene, the best single click in the deck.
- **Chilled** opens the morning Tilly comes back.
- **Betrayed** opens the betrayal pair with Rinn's "darkness" line.
- **Exhilarated** shows Jenny's "believing you were free" nearby.
- **Content** and **Quietly defiant** are best simply read.
- **Suggestion:** read the arc aloud first. If you click once, click **Bored**.

## Frame 8: Wants Made for Us

- The smoothie opens with *"You always know best"* just below it.
- The dessert opens the date scene (same as frame 4).
- The leisure regimen and *"Do you even know what you really want any more?"* stand alone; the slide already gives the whole point.
- **Suggestion:** read through. Click the dessert only if the date scene hasn't been shown yet.

## Frame 9: Watched for Profit

- **The paid pitch** opens Jenny's apartment debate.
- *"Gaps in sharing"* opens the date scene again.
- *"If it can't be found…"* opens the end of Rinn's speech.
- **Suggestion:** click the paid pitch. It is the one scene not reachable from any other main frame.

## Frame 10: What Keeps You Human?

- *"He was on his own"* shows Jenny's *"You grew up believing you were free…"* nearby.
- **Suggestion:** optional. The slide's point carries without the click; the question after the pause matters more.

## Frame 11: Control Dressed as Care

- *"Tilly, call the police!"* opens the capture scene.
- *"I'm being treated like a child"* opens the date scene again.
- **Suggestion:** click the police line. Seeing the white tea right below Tilly's refusal makes the point without a word of explanation.

## Frame 12: Controllers' Treatment of Rebels

- All six quotes sit within a few screens of Rinn's speech.
- **Suggestion:** click **"I want you to come and work for Centillion"** once; it sits in the middle of the cluster. Scroll up and down rather than clicking the others.

## Frame 13: A Victory, a Surrender, or Both?

- The red light opens with "My Way" just above it.
- **Suggestion:** worth the click if time allows. Reading the last few lines aloud, in context, is a natural way to end.

## Extra slides

- **Surveillance Capitalism 3** (the twelve-hour gap) and **What Keeps You Human 2** (Jenny's "believing you were free") open clusters already covered by frames 3, 4 and 10.
- **Suggestion:** read; click only if you're already on that slide.

# If time is short

- **Four clicks:** the date scene (frame 4, Bored), the paid pitch (frame 9), the capture (frame 11), and Rinn's offer (frame 12).
- **Two clicks:** the date scene and Rinn's offer, the beginning and the end of Tilly's hold on Sai.
- **None:** every slide still makes its point from the quote on the slide itself. The links are there for when the discussion wants the scene.
